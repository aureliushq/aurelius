# Aurelius

Aurelius is a place where writers just write. Every other feature serves the writing, and each one is optional. This glossary fixes the words for the product's people, agents and promises.

## Language

### People

**Writer**:
The person Aurelius is made for: someone who writes and owns that writing. A writer is not assumed to be technical or to know Markdown.
_Avoid_: Author, owner, customer

**Collaborator**:
A person a writer invites to work on a piece of writing. A collaborator is always a person, never an agent.
_Avoid_: Team member, teammate, guest

### Agents

**Agent**:
Software driven by an AI model that reads or changes writing for a writer.
_Avoid_: AI, assistant, bot

**Agent panel**:
The place inside Aurelius where a writer works with an agent. It is on only after the writer connects a provider.
_Avoid_: Built-in agent chat, chat, copilot

**Outside agent**:
An agent that runs outside Aurelius and connects to it.
_Avoid_: External agent, third-party agent

**Provider**:
A hosted AI model service that a writer connects so that the agent panel can work.
_Avoid_: Model, vendor

### Promises

**Agent-agnostic**:
The promise that no agent and no provider is favoured: every agent, in the agent panel or outside, has the same abilities.
_Avoid_: Agent-neutral, model-agnostic

**Local-first**:
The promise that every feature that needs no other device and no other person works with no account and no network, and that writing leaves the device only when the writer acts.
_Avoid_: Offline-first, offline mode

**Optional**:
The property of every feature other than writing itself: the writer can ignore it and still write. An optional feature may show itself; it may not stand in the way.
_Avoid_: Opt-in, hidden
